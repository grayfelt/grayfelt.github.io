Hello, again

May 22, 2025

I have republished my website, which once again includes blog entries and a gallery of my creations. I much prefer sharing the things I care about through a website—it gives me complete control over the format and aesthetic, which, as you can see, I enjoy manipulating. In the near future, you might witness the continuation of my many hobbies: painting, drawing, photography, and perhaps even embroidery, if I can muster the will. I may also write about my adventures here in Estonia, the memories I create travelling, or the things I care about. Who knows, if I continue learning Estonian you might have to start translating!

I miss you all, and I hope you are doing well. Come see me in Tallinn, the summer is beautiful here. I have an apartment (with a free couch) outside of the old town, and I am surrounded by wonderful friends.
I hope to see you soon.
